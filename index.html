How it works

Assign → Submit → Review → Track

A simple four-step loop replaces manual follow-up at every handoff.

1 · Assign

Manager assigns instructor tasks with course + deadline. Instructors break work into student tasks per lesson.

2 · Submit

Instructors and students submit deliverables. Status moves Pending → In Progress → Submitted.

3 · Review

Reviewers approve, request revision, or mark Under Review → Completed. Every transition is logged.

4 · Track

Dashboards, history and delay alerts give live visibility — overdue is flagged automatically.

Business rules BR-001 → BR-010

Guardrails that keep the workflow honest

Condensed from the specification into five enforceable groups.

BR-001 · BR-002 — Required fields & valid deadlinesTasks require title, assignee, course and a future deadline. Past dates are rejected.
BR-003 · BR-004 — Controlled status flowOnly allowed transitions (e.g. Submitted → Under Review → Completed / Revision Required). All changes append to history.
BR-005 · BR-006 — Auto overdue detectionPast-deadline, incomplete tasks flip to Overdue automatically and raise a notification.
BR-007 · BR-008 — Role permissionsManagers manage instructors; instructors manage their students; students can only submit their own tasks.
BR-009 · BR-010 — Progress integrityProgress % stays 0–100 and derives from subtask completion; reports compute from live data only.
